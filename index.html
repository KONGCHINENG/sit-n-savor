Our story

Food and coffee at a relax tea place.

Sit N Savor is a neighbourhood cafe in Greentown Business Centre. The idea is in the name: come in, take a seat, and enjoy a proper meal without hurrying and without paying too much.

Our menu covers grilled chicken chop rice with eight sauces, katsu pork chop, fried rice, aglio olio, burgers, croissants and snacks, plus fresh juices, coffee and tea. Eating alone? Our 个人也可以吃的好 single meal sets come with everything you need on one tray.
